# Lori anatomical likeness study: provenance

The head and hands are edited derivatives of MakeHuman Community anatomical assets at revision `a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482`:
https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/tree/a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482

Sources: `makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj`, female macrodetails and facial targets under `makehuman/data/targets`, and expression units under `expression/units/caucasian`. Asset headers explicitly release these meshes and targets under CC0. See `LICENSE-MAKEHUMAN-CC0.md`. The authors credited by the upstream assets are Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier, and Manuel Bastioni.

Clothing, layered wave hair, accessory details and presentation are authored procedural geometry. No third-party hair models, photographed skin maps or copyrighted reference photographs are bundled. The supplied private portrait was visually inspected to guide proportions, hairstyle and black asymmetric neckline. Lower-body clothing is an interpretation because the portrait does not show it.

This is an editable stylized anatomical likeness study, not a scan, an endorsement, or a claim of photorealistic identity. CC0 source permissions do not grant personality, publicity, trademark or photograph rights.
