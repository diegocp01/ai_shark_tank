# Barbara Corcoran character study

The head and anatomical hands derive from this repository's bundled CC0 MakeHuman data: `assets/lori/anatomical-head.npz` and `assets/lori/source/anatomical_hands_cc0.npz`. The head is reshaped in `scripts/barbara_character.py`; its vertex pigment, UVs and expression topology are preserved. A single subdivision is baked consistently into all head shapes for the runtime export.

The pinned upstream revision is `a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482` of [MakeHuman Community](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/tree/a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482). Credited authors: Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier and Manuel Bastioni. See [the bundled CC0 license](../lori/LICENSE-MAKEHUMAN-CC0.md) and [original provenance](../lori/PROVENANCE.md).

The cropped layered hair, red wrap dress, pearls, earrings, bracelet, body tailoring and shoe styling are procedural geometry authored for this study, with shared body helpers from `scripts/lori_body.py`. No model-generation service, downloaded hair or skin photograph is used. The supplied reference image guided the visible styling and is not redistributed. The unseen shoes and rear view are interpretations.

This is a stylized likeness study, not a scan or an endorsement. The source geometry's CC0 license does not grant rights to the subject's likeness or the reference photograph.
